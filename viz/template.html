Terrella — storm replay

A learned simulator of Earth's magnetosphere, replaying the storm. The same real solar wind is fired at the model four times, each at a magnetosphere left in a different starting condition by a preceding 24 hours of driving. Everything below the first panel is the model's output — including the hidden state, which no instrument on Earth can measure.
